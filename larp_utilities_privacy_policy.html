LARP Utilities — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LARP Utilities (the “Bot”), used in connection with Los Angeles Roleplay (“LARP”) Discord communities, may handle information when you interact with it.

1. Information the Bot may process

Depending on which features are enabled, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs needed to identify where a command was used and apply server settings.
  • Command names, command options, and interaction data needed to respond to commands such as session start, shutdown, vote, boost, low, and full.
  • Session, vote, or server-configuration information created through the Bot, if those features are enabled and the information is stored.
  • Basic technical or error information needed to diagnose failures and keep the Bot operating.

The Bot is not intended to request real names, home addresses, passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive personal information. Do not submit such information through Bot commands.

2. How information is used

Information is used only as needed to operate Bot commands, manage roleplay sessions and votes, maintain server configuration, troubleshoot errors, and protect the Bot against abuse or misuse.

3. Storage and retention

Information may be processed in memory or stored by the Bot’s hosting provider or database, depending on the features enabled. Stored information should be kept only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above, then deleted when reasonably possible. The Bot owner should review the deployed code and hosting configuration to confirm exactly what is stored and for how long.

4. Sharing

The Bot owner does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and by infrastructure providers used to host or operate the Bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or the associated Discord community.

5. Discord and third-party services

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The Bot does not control Discord’s own data practices or those of its hosting providers.

6. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect Bot credentials and stored information. Access to the Bot token, hosting dashboard, and any database should be restricted to authorized operators. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

If you have a question about information processed by the Bot, or want to request deletion of information the Bot owner controls, contact the owner or administrator of the Discord server where you used the Bot. Requests may be limited by technical requirements, legitimate moderation needs, or legal obligations.

8. Children

The Bot is intended for use in accordance with Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable law. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children outside those requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Bot’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the LARP Utilities Bot owner through the official LARP Discord community.

Important for the Bot owner: This is a starter policy based on the Bot’s planned session and voting commands. Before publishing, make sure it matches the actual code, database, logs, and hosting setup. Replace the contact method with a reliable contact channel if you have one.
